One-line: renders the official Medartis wordmark (lowercase, geometric) or the figure symbol from assets/logo.

```jsx
<Logo width={111} assetBase="../../assets/logo" />
<Logo tone="white" />
<Logo variant="symbol" width={64} />
```

On slides the wordmark is always 111×19px, 40px from the top and 26.67px from the right edge. The symbol artwork exists only in white — place it on black or on a photograph.
